The React render cycle

The corpus holds concept articles and symptom-first recipes in separate trees, and read separately each half is weaker than it should be. A concept article explains a mechanism with no bug in front of it; a recipe diagnoses a bug and points back at a mechanism the reader has not met yet. This sequence alternates them.

The order is bottom-up. It starts with what JSX becomes, because everything after it is about when that output is recomputed. The Rules of React sit after the render model rather than before it, because the constraints only make sense once you know what they protect.
